# Cheesy Pizza privacy policy

_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

This policy covers the Cheesy Pizza Android app and website, run by Cheesy Pizza, a pure veg
pizza shop in Gujarat, India.

## What we collect

- **Your account:** name, mobile number and a 4-digit PIN (stored only as a scrambled hash,
  never as the PIN itself). Your birthday and a friend's referral code only if you choose to add them.
- **Your orders:** what you order, the outlet, order type (takeaway, delivery, dine-in), delivery
  address if you give one, payment method, and order times.
- **Rewards:** your Cheese Coins, wallet balance and referral code.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any data from other apps. The app does not
show ads and has no advertising or analytics trackers.

## How we use it

Only to take and deliver your orders, show your order history, run your wallet and Cheese Coins,
and contact you about an order (for example by phone or WhatsApp when you ask us to send a bill).

## Where it is stored

Orders and accounts are stored on the Cheesy Pizza shop's own server. If you use a version of the
app that works offline, this data stays on your phone only. We do not sell or share your data with
anyone. When you choose to share on WhatsApp, you send that message yourself through WhatsApp.

## Your choices

You can use the menu and order as a guest without an account. To change or delete your account and
its data, call the outlet on 77780 43066 and we will do it.

## Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not aimed at children under 13.
